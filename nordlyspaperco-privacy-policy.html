Privacy Policy — Nordlys Paper Co. Pin Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Nordlys Paper Co. Pin Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by Nordlys Paper Co., a small shop selling printable digital products on Etsy. It publishes Pins with images of our own products to our own Pinterest account. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Our own Pinterest account and boards — to create Pins on our boards.
  • OAuth access tokens issued by Pinterest for our account, stored locally on our computer.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data of any other Pinterest users.
  • It does not read other users' Pins, boards, followers or messages.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Data retention

Access tokens are kept only as long as needed to publish our Pins and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings. A local log of published Pin IDs is kept to avoid duplicate posts.
